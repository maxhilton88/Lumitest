💰 Currency System 2 currencies
🪙 Gold
Earnable
Earned through all activities. Buys consumables, common/rare items. The primary daily loop currency.
💎 Diamond
Premium
Earned via referrals & milestones only. Buys epic/legendary items and rare shop exclusives.
⭐ XP
Progress
Not a spendable currency — drives leveling. High XP = higher battle stats + better rewards per question.
❤️ HP
Resource
Depletes in battle. Restored via potions from shop. Gate for battle entry (≥10% max HP required).
💎 Diamond Earning Paths (free)
Diamonds are aspirational but earnable without paying:
• 5 💎 on signup (existing)  |  • 3 💎 per successful paid referral
• 1 💎 per free account referral  |  • 10 💎 reaching Level 10 milestone
• 1 💎 for each weekly perfect score challenge

🕸️ Spider Web — How it maps to rewards
Each skill has a Kod Kemahiran (e.g. MAT-T1-N01). Every activity (test/practice/flashcard/battle) is tagged to 1+ skill codes.

Mastery scoring per skill:
• 0 attempts → ◉ Unstarted
• 1–2 correct → ◉ Exploring
• 3+ correct, avg ≥60% → ◉ Learning
• 5+ correct, avg ≥80% → ◉ Proficient
• 10+ correct, avg ≥90% → ◉ Mastered

Parent spider chart shows mastery % per subject per year level. Weak nodes (Unstarted/Exploring) are surfaced in the daily quest recommendation engine — nudging kids to study gaps.
✅ Reward for mastering a skill node
When a skill goes from Learning → Proficient: +20 XP bonus + "Skill Unlocked" animation.
When a skill reaches Mastered: +50 XP + subject-specific badge (collectible) + gold bonus.
👥 Referral System (Streamlined) One mechanism for all users
Single referral link — works for both parents and kids
Every user (parent or child account) gets one referral link/code. The reward scales based on what the referred person does after signing up. No separate KG referral mechanism.
Referral OutcomeWho earns💎 Gems💰 Cash Credit
Referred person signs up free account Referrer +1 💎 —
Referred person subscribes to Plan A (RM365/yr) Referrer +3 💎 RM36.50 credit
Referred person subscribes to Plan B (RM730) Referrer +5 💎 RM36.50 credit
Referrer reaches 10 paid referrals (milestone) Referrer +20 💎 1 year free (RM365 credit)
💡 Key rules
• Single level only — no MLM, only direct referrer is rewarded
• No KG-specific referral awards — KG users use the same mechanism as everyone else
• Credits accumulate in referral_credits on the parent record (existing)
• Free → Paid upgrade counts: if a referred free user later upgrades, referrer gets the paid reward delta automatically
• Show a Referral Dashboard in-app: link copied, # invited, # signed up, # paid, total gems + credits earned
