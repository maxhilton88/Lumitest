Updated battle damage formula — spirits share Lumi's level
baseDamage = movePower × (baseATK / 100)
baseATK = 10 + (lumiLevel × 3) + equip_ATK
typeMod = typeEffectiveness(attackerType, defenderType) // 0.25 | 0.5 | 1 | 2 | 4
spiritMod = spirit.baseStatModifier // e.g. Fire ATK ×1.15
speedMod = answerSpeed // 1.0 | 1.15 | 1.3
rawDamage = baseDamage × typeMod × spiritMod × speedMod
netDamage = max(1, rawDamage − equip_DEF)
Example — Lumi Level 10, Fire spirit vs Wood enemy
baseATK = 10 + (10×3) = 40
movePower = Flameburst = 65
baseDamage = 65 × (40/100) = 26
typeMod = Fire vs Wood = 2×
spiritMod = Fire ATK ×1.15 = 1.15×
speedMod = answered in 2s = 1.3×
rawDamage = 26 × 2 × 1.15 × 1.3 = 77.74 → 78
netDamage (no DEF gear) = 78 damage
Wood enemy HP at Level 10 = 200 HP → ~2–3 hits to KO ✅
